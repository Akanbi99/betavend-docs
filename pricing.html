Billing

  • Plan fee is charged monthly in advance to your Betavend wallet
  • Overages are deducted from your wallet in real time as they occur
  • At 80% of your plan limit you receive an email warning
  • At 100% usage, requests are throttled until the next billing cycle or you upgrade
  • Invoices are auto-generated and downloadable from business.betavend.com/billing
